Welcome to the Solar System

Mercury | Venus | Earth | Mars | Jupiter | Saturn | Uranus | Neptune

Mercury

Mercury is the closest planet to the Sun and the smallest in our solar system. It has a rocky surface and experiences extreme temperature variations.

Venus

Venus is the second planet from the Sun and is known for its thick, toxic atmosphere that traps heat, making it the hottest planet in our solar system.

Earth

Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the only known planet to support life. It has a diverse climate and surface, with 70% of its surface covered by water.

Mars

Mars, the fourth planet from the Sun, is often called the "Red Planet" due to its reddish appearance. It has the largest volcano and canyon in the solar system.

Jupiter

Jupiter is the largest planet in our solar system and is known for its Great Red Spot, a giant storm that has been raging for centuries. It has a strong magnetic field and dozens of moons.

Saturn

Saturn is the sixth planet from the Sun and is known for its stunning rings made of ice and rock particles. It is a gas giant with a thick atmosphere.
